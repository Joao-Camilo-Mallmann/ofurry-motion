# clean-editorial-motion Specification

## Purpose
Esthetic standard for OFurry motion graphics enforcing clean editorial presentation, ultra-minimal typography, and visceral physical metaphors directly over native alpha without opaque dashboard containers.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Anti-SaaS Dashboard and Anti-AI Aesthetic
Motion scenes SHALL NOT utilize opaque background cards, secondary UI metadata badges, or multi-line descriptive text paragraphs.

#### Scenario: Visual purity on native alpha
- **WHEN** elements are composed on screen
- **THEN** monumental typography and kinetic vectors float directly over the transparent Alpha channel without enclosed grey container cards or dashboard borders.

### Requirement: Ultra-Minimal Keyword Ceiling (2 to 4 Words)
Every motion scene SHALL restrict text display to 2 to 4 monumental anchor words or metrics.

#### Scenario: Minimalist typography enforcement
- **WHEN** text elements are authored for a scene
- **THEN** sentences and paragraphs are discarded in favor of 2 to 4 anchor keywords sized between 110px and 180px.

### Requirement: Pure Physical Kinetic Metaphors
Motion scenes SHALL translate narrative tension into direct physical kinematics (laser slicing, ceiling collision, asset decay, and explosive pulse locks).

#### Scenario: Ceiling collision for gain limits
- **WHEN** a ceiling or cap is represented (e.g., Call Spread)
- **THEN** the metric/asset line rises and physically collides with an impenetrable horizontal barrier producing impact sparks while the underlying market continues climbing.
